# whoami

David Rodenas, PhD. Barcelona.

I started writing programs at eight, on a ZX Spectrum, with a book called
*Basic para niños*. Before university I had written a neural net and a ray
tracer. Since then: a supercomputing centre, a graph database kernel, a
startup I co-founded, seven years of university teaching, and nine years on
one platform in travel tech.

I have written more refactors than features. I have spent long enough on the
same system to meet my own decisions coming back.

- 254 essays, one every Saturday since 2022, without missing one
- A book, *The Emotional and Technical Guide to Rescue Stalled Software* (2024)
- 34 npm packages; two of them still in production use a decade later
- 266 public repositories, and about twenty-five years of public code
